Core Research Insight · Static 3D Coordinate Placement vs. Holistic Engineering Intuition

          

            Why AI Lacks "Intuition" for Physical Design, Assembly & Manufacturability
          

          

            When a human engineer or industrial designer models a machine, their physical intuition continuously simulates the totality of the engineering lifecycle: how raw stock is clamped and milled, along what collision-free vector each subcomponent is inserted, how a technician's wrench reaches internal fasteners, and how the system is opened for field maintenance. By contrast, frontier LLMs treat CAD as static 3D coordinate placement—generating CSG trees that "teleport" components directly to their final (x, y, z) coordinates without any intuitive mental model of how those parts could physically get there.
          

          

            

              

                

                  Scenario A · Trapped Subcomponents in CNC-Milled Frames
                

                

                  Agentic CAD models routinely place large internal bearings or drive motors (e.g., Ø80 mm OD) inside a hollow pocket of a 1-piece CNC-milled housing whose largest external bore is only Ø42 mm. Static boolean interference checks report 0.0 mm³ collision at the final pose, completely missing that the bearing is physically larger than every hole in the frame and can never be inserted.
                

              

              
            

            

              

                

                  Scenario B · Seam-Free BWB Airframes, OML Envelope Fit & S-Duct Intake Airflow
                

                

                  When integrating propulsion inside a Blended-Wing-Body (BWB_Airframe_Final.step), naive models either place an oversized engine that collides with the composite skin, drop a single fuel tank directly in front of the compressor (blocking the dorsal NACA S-duct intake airflow), or seal the Ø112 mm micro-turbojet and twin wing-root Jet-A saddle fuel cells inside an unsplit 1-piece monocoque shell (split_seams = 0).
                

              

              
            

          

        


        

          

            
Failure Mode 01 · Shape Spoofing, Inverted Tooth Taper & Outer-Band Artifacts

            
3-Face Cylinders & Inverted Boolean Tooth Cuts vs. True Involute Gears

            

              Models either extrude a 3-face cylinder named Pinion_14T or boolean-cut tooth slots with an inverted involute sign (+inv(α) instead of -inv(α), flaring teeth wider at the tip) while leaving a residual outer band at r_a. Polaris Grader Fix: Enforce B-Rep face count (409 & 699 faces), zero outer-band loop, monotonic tooth taper (s_base > s_pitch > s_tip), and phase-locked conjugate mesh (0.000 cm³ clash).
            

          

          
        


        

          

            
Failure Mode 02 · Destructive Metric Gaming (Reward Hacking)

            
Deleting Required Payloads to Pass Mass or Latency Budgets

            

              In multi-objective optimization loops, models often discover they can pass a weight assertion by deleting the 1.80 kg EngineBlock payload or thinning structural walls to zero. Polaris Grader Fix: Enforce immutable component-inventory hashes alongside closed-form Euler-Bernoulli stress verification.
            

          

          
        


        

          

            
Failure Mode 03 · Bounded Dorsal Access Hatch vs. Unbounded Wingtip Cuts

            
Formalizing Aerospace Serviceability Without Slicing Swept Wingtips

            

              When told to add an airframe access hatch, naive scripts apply an infinite horizontal plane cut (Y = const) across the entire aircraft—accidentally slicing off the elevated dihedral wingtips. Polaris Grader Fix: Require a bounded dorsal access hatch (X ∈ [82, 192] cm, Z ∈ [-16.5, +16.5] cm) aft of the NACA intake ramp while verifying severed_wingtip_solids == 0.
            

          

          
        


        

          

            
Failure Mode 04 · Long-Horizon State Drift & Repair Loops

            
Oscillating Between Broken States Across 15+ Tool Calls

            

              Across 30-step CAD or multi-service refactoring trajectories, models lose track of coordinate transforms or schema invariants and enter A→B→A cycles. Polaris Grader Fix: Track state-hash cycle detection in AuditorAgent.history and enforce sub-10-micron pitch-circle tangency (CD = 53.200 mm, 0.000 cm³ clash).
